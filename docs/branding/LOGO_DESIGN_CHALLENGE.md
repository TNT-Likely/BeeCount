## 1. Why a refresh? / 为什么重新设计？

BeeCount is an open-source personal finance and bookkeeping app. Version 4.0 is a good moment to revisit the visual identity and build a consistent brand system for the app, website, store listings, and community materials.

BeeCount 是一款开源个人记账应用。4.0 正式上线后，我们希望重新审视现有 Logo，建立一套更统一的品牌视觉体系，覆盖 App、官网、应用商店和社区传播素材。

The current bee mark is friendly, but feels closer to a generic cartoon illustration than a distinctive product identity. We want to keep the warmth of the BeeCount name while making the mark simpler, more memorable, and more versatile.

目前的蜜蜂图形比较亲切，但更接近普通卡通插画，作为产品品牌标识还不够鲜明。我们希望保留 BeeCount 名称带来的亲切感，同时让图形更简洁、更容易记住，也更适合不同使用场景。

## 2. Design goals / 设计目标

A strong proposal should:

- Be recognizable at small sizes such as 24 × 24 px.
- Work as an app icon on iOS and Android, and remain suitable for desktop, web, and promotional materials.
- Feel friendly and approachable, while communicating trust, clarity, and order.
- Work in monochrome and on both light and dark backgrounds.
- Have a clear relationship to BeeCount without relying on tiny details or generic finance symbols.
- Be practical to maintain as a long-term brand asset.

优秀方案需要：

- 在 24 × 24 px 等小尺寸下仍然清晰、有辨识度。
- 适用于 iOS、Android App 图标，也能延展到桌面端、Web 和宣传物料。
- 既亲切友好，也能传达可信赖、清晰、有秩序的感觉。
- 能够适配单色、浅色背景和深色背景。
- 与 BeeCount 有明确联系，但不要依赖过多细节或简单拼凑通用金融符号。
- 适合作为长期维护的品牌资产。

## 3. Open directions / 开放的设计方向

These are starting points, not restrictions. A completely different direction is welcome.

以下只是启发，不是限制。欢迎提出完全不同的创意方向。

1. **Abstract bee + bookkeeping / 抽象蜜蜂 + 记账元素** — retain a subtle bee silhouette and combine it with ideas such as recording, pages, lists, or balance.
2. **BeeCount monogram / 字母标识** — build a distinctive symbol around the letter B, integrating wings, stripes, or honeycomb geometry.
3. **Honeycomb + data / 蜂巢 + 数据秩序** — use geometric structure to express accumulation, organization, and financial clarity.
4. **A new direction / 自由创作** — reinterpret the BeeCount name and product personality in your own way.

The existing yellow/black palette can be a reference, but it is not mandatory. A refreshed palette is welcome if it has a clear rationale and works across platforms.

现有黄黑配色可以作为参考，但不是硬性要求。如果有更合适的配色方案，也欢迎提出，并说明设计理由及跨平台适配方式。

## 4. What to submit / 投稿内容

A complete proposal should include as many of the following as possible:

- **Primary mark**: the core symbol, preferably editable vector artwork.
- **App icon**: a 1024 × 1024 px preview, with a note about safe area and background treatment.
- **Wordmark**: BeeCount, and optionally the Chinese name 蜜蜂记账.
- **Variants**: icon-only, horizontal/stacked lockup, monochrome, light-background, and dark-background versions.
- **Usage previews**: at least one app-icon mockup and one brand/application mockup.
- **Design rationale**: a short explanation of the idea, shape, color, and typography.
- **Source**: a Figma link with view access, or editable SVG/source files.

尽可能提交以下内容：

- **核心图形**：可编辑的矢量设计稿。
- **App 图标**：1024 × 1024 px 预览，并说明安全区和背景处理。
- **品牌字标**：BeeCount 字样，可选附带中文名「蜜蜂记账」。
- **衍生版本**：纯图形、横版/竖版组合、单色、浅色背景和深色背景版本。
- **应用效果**：至少提供一张 App 图标效果图和一张品牌应用场景效果图。
- **设计说明**：简要介绍创意、图形、配色和字体思路。
- **源文件**：可公开查看的 Figma 链接，或可编辑 SVG/源文件。

**Preferred formats / 推荐格式**: SVG for vector artwork; PNG for previews; Figma for editable source. Please ensure font and third-party asset licenses allow the intended use.

矢量稿优先使用 SVG，预览图使用 PNG，可编辑源文件优先使用 Figma。请确保字体和第三方素材的许可允许预期用途。

4. Please do not open an implementation PR against the app before a design is selected. We will coordinate implementation with the selected designer.

参与步骤：

4. 方案入选前，请不要直接向 App 仓库提交 Logo 替换 PR；后续会与入选设计者一起协调落地。

- Submission does **not** automatically transfer copyright or grant production, commercial, trademark, or exclusive-use rights to BeeCount.
- Before a selected design is used in the app, website, store listings, or promotional materials, the designer and project maintainer will agree in writing on the necessary license/authorization, modification rights, attribution, and any exclusivity or transfer terms.
- Please disclose third-party assets, fonts, stock resources, and AI-assisted work. All included materials must have licenses compatible with the intended use.

- 入选方案用于 App、官网、应用商店或宣传物料前，设计者与项目维护者会另行书面确认所需授权、修改权、署名方式，以及是否涉及独占授权或版权转让。
